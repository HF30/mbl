MBL Multifamily Education

Methods for evaluating rental property and small development projects

MBL Developments offers education and underwriting tools for Canadian multifamily real estate. Its programs include CMHC MLI Select for Existing Multifamily, a Canada-wide course for evaluating and preparing the financing file for an existing five-or-more-unit property, and Toronto Sixplex Development & CMHC Financing, a Toronto-specific course and Deal Lab covering a project from lot screening through lender preparation.

Two different property decisions. One program addresses CMHC financing for an existing multifamily building anywhere in Canada. The other addresses the acquisition, development and financing of a new six-unit rental building in Toronto. The objective of both is not a favourable answer — it is assembled evidence, reconciled calculations, and a clear view of what remains subject to professional review. Some properties proceed. Others should be passed on.

Current programs

Existing multifamily · Canada-wide

CMHC MLI Select for Existing Multifamily

For people evaluating an existing rental property.

The course covers the documents, calculations and questions required to assess a multifamily acquisition: the rent roll, operating history, normalized NOI, valuation, loan sizing, cash requirement, downside scenarios and due diligence.

Students apply the process to a sample property or their own candidate using the Deal Screen.

Final output: An Acquisition Decision Memo supporting an acquire, renegotiate or pass conclusion.

Format: Self-paced course and CMHC Financing Screen Full course page → Status: In development Planned fee: C$297

Toronto Sixplex Development & CMHC Financing

First Project, Financed — the MBL course and Deal Lab

For owners and buyers evaluating a new five-or-more-unit rental development in Toronto.

The course follows a project file from initial property screening through rent evidence, preliminary building program, development costs, maximum bid, CMHC MLI Select strategy, monthly cash requirements, due diligence and lender preparation.

The Deal Lab maintains one project record across the course. Calculations are deterministic, evidence is attached to the relevant assumptions, and unresolved items remain visible.

Final output: A development assessment and lender-review package — or a documented decision not to proceed.

Format: Guided course and six months of Deal Lab access Status: In production Planned fee: C$1,500

Toronto Multiplex Project Review

For graduates of First Project, Financed who have an active property under evaluation and want their work challenged before approaching the relevant professionals and lenders.

This is a structured twelve-week review of the participant's submitted property file. The review covers the lot assessment, rent evidence, preliminary program, budget, financing assumptions, cash schedule, due-diligence status, risk register and lender package.

Admission depends on the readiness of the file, the project timeline and whether the proposed work falls within the program's scope.

Final output: A revised professional-review package, a defined repositioning plan or a documented pass.

Format: Limited-capacity group program with scheduled private file reviews Fee: C$10,000

How the work is handled

Evidence before assumptions

Material figures are linked to their source or clearly identified as estimates. Missing evidence is not silently replaced by an AI-generated answer.

One project record

Rents, areas, costs and financing assumptions are entered once and carried through every related calculation and document.

Calculations that can be checked

Financial calculations are deterministic and visible. AI is used to organize information, identify inconsistencies and explain results — not to invent comparables or approve a project.

Professional review remains professional review

The programs help participants prepare and examine their files. They do not replace architectural, engineering, planning, legal, tax, appraisal or lending advice, and they do not guarantee financing or approval.

Free tool: the CMHC Multifamily Quick Screen sizes an existing-property loan under DCR and LTV and names the binding constraint — free, in the browser, nothing stored. And the Financing Readiness Workbook — nine worksheets with live formulas, free with your email.
