The MBL Deal Lab

Our operating system,
taught the way we run it.

MBL is a Toronto missing-middle developer. We buy single-family lots in the as-of-right sixplex wards and replace them with six-unit CMHC-financed rentals. The education below is not a business beside that work — it is the same acquisition, underwriting and financing process we use on our own projects, productized so you can run it on yours. Every check is deterministic, every number needs a source, and a documented pass is a successful outcome.

Three ways in, in order of depth

Start here — existing buildings

Multifamily Underwriting Fundamentals

C$297 · founding cohort C$197 · credited toward First Project, Financed

Learn to underwrite an existing multifamily rental property — rent roll, normalized operating statement, real NOI, valuation, DCR/LTV loan sizing, downside testing and due diligence — and finish with a defensible proceed, renegotiate or pass decision. Includes the Deal Screen: a deterministic analyzer for acquisitions. No development content; that lives one level up.

The flagship — build a project

First Project, Financed — course & Deal Lab

C$1,500 · entry-course tuition credited on upgrade

Build a lender-review-ready Toronto multiplex package: lot screening, closed-comp rent underwriting, cost stack, max-bid discipline, CMHC MLI Select structuring, cash planning and lender submission — inside the same Deal Lab workflow we use to screen our own acquisitions. You finish with a proceed package or a documented pass, either of which is worth more than optimism.

Live Deal Advisory

C$10,000 · small cohort · full course tuition credited

For graduates with a real property under evaluation: our team reviews your actual file — the rent evidence, the budget, the MLI pathway, the lender package — the way we red-team our own. Application-only; we take few projects, and we say no when the deal or the fit is wrong. Advisory is where the public ladder ends: it makes your project stronger, it is not a route into ours.
