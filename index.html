The Opportunity

The missing middle

Toronto quietly made its biggest zoning change in generations: six rental units, as-of-right, on regular residential lots in nine wards. No rezoning. No committee fights. Development charges waived.

Families priced out of buying want to live in these neighbourhoods — The Beaches, Danforth Village, Leslieville, East York — yet almost no family-sized rental supply exists. That gap is our work.

The Engine

Every decision runs on evidence

Behind every acquisition is a proprietary underwriting engine — AI-assisted analysis, human-verified decisions, and a discipline most buyers can't match.

01

Closed data, not asking prices

We track thousands of closed leases and sales across ten MLS districts — refreshed monthly. Rents are underwritten against what tenants actually signed, never what landlords hoped.

02

A maximum bid for every lot

Every ward and every property gets its own printed ceiling from the buy box — finished value, build cost, financing, all solved before we offer. When a property goes over our number, someone else owns it.

03

Financing engineered in

Projects are designed to CMHC MLI Select's 100+ point tier from the first sketch — energy and affordability built in, unlocking the strongest rental-housing financing in Canada.
