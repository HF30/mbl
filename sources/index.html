Sources & Citations

Everything we publish — the videos, the ward guides, the numbers — traces back to a primary source. Here they are, in one place. Rules, charges, rents and program criteria change. These links point to the official current versions; always confirm what applies to your project on the date you act. Nothing here is financial, legal, tax or mortgage advice.

Toronto zoning — multiplexes & sixplexes

  • City of Toronto — Multiplex Housing
    Up to four residential units permitted as-of-right in most low-rise residential areas city-wide (since May 2023), subject to the applicable zoning standards.
  • Sixplex permission — Zoning By-law 654-2025
    Up to six units as-of-right on an eligible detached lot, in the Toronto & East York Community Council area plus Ward 23 (Scarborough North) — the "nine wards." Subject to zoning, building type and performance standards.
  • Province of Ontario — Bill 23, More Homes Built Faster Act, 2022
    The provincial framework enabling additional residential units and multiplex reform.

Development charges

  • City of Toronto — Development Charges Overview
    How DCs are calculated and what rate applies to a project.
  • 2025 small-multiplex DC exemption (Council items EX24.2 / MM32.5)
    Effective July 24, 2025: the City exempts the 2nd through 6th units in developments of up to six units from municipal development charges (the first/base unit still applies). Education levies, parkland, permit fees and servicing costs are separate — verify each. As of Nov 3, 2025, non-rental residential DCs are payable at occupancy, not permit issuance.

CMHC financing — MLI Select

  • CMHC — MLI Select (program page)
    Multi-unit mortgage-loan insurance for rental of five or more units, scored on affordability, energy efficiency and accessibility.
  • CMHC — MLI Select fact sheet (PDF)
    The point tiers and their maximums (e.g., 50 / 70 / 100 points → up to 40 / 45 / 50-year amortization; up to 95% loan-to-cost for new construction at tier). These are maximums, not approvals — a CMHC-approved lender determines eligibility and terms; borrower net-worth and liquidity requirements apply.

Market data

    Toronto average rents by unit type and vacancy forecasts (the source for our rent figures; we cite the report and vintage).
  • CBRE — Canada Real Estate Insights

Why we don't give financing advice

  • FSRA — Mortgage Brokering in Ontario
    Assessing borrowers, recommending, and arranging mortgages are regulated activities. MBL Developments is a developer, not a licensed mortgage brokerage. We explain how programs work; financing eligibility and advice come from a CMHC-approved lender or a licensed Ontario mortgage professional.
