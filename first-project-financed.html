First Project, Financed — the MBL course and Deal Lab

You are not buying information. You are building the file your project needs.

A guided course and Deal Lab that produces a source-linked, lender-review-ready Toronto multiplex deal package — from lot screening and rent underwriting through CMHC MLI strategy, cash planning and lender submission. You finish with one of two things: a defensible proceed package, or a documented pass decision explaining exactly why the property fails. A good pass is a successful outcome — this course will not help you talk yourself into a bad deal.

The Deal Lab — specialized agents, deterministic math

Alongside the lessons, a set of purpose-built AI agents helps assemble and audit your documents: a lot screener, a rent analyst that refuses to invent comps, a budget scope checker that finds what your quote omitted, a deterministic pro-forma engine, an MLI file builder with dated sources, a cash-and-draw planner — and a red-team agent whose only job is to try to reject your package before a lender does. Every output separates verified facts from your assumptions from open items. The system never says "approved" — the best grade it gives is ready for professional review.

Who it's for

  • First-time and early developers pursuing a Toronto multiplex
  • Owners of a candidate property who want the real math before deciding
  • Investors and agents who want to underwrite this asset class properly

Who it's not for

  • Anyone looking for "no money down" secrets — real projects require real equity, and we'll show you exactly how much
  • Anyone wanting a guaranteed approval — financing is never guaranteed; this course builds the strongest possible file and teaches you to walk away from weak ones

The practical details

  • Time commitment: ~5–6 hours of concise video; a serious student spends 15–25 hours total building their package
  • Deal Lab access: six months, including three preliminary property screens and one complete deal-package build with revisions
  • Geography: built for Toronto (zoning, data and program specifics); the underwriting method itself is portable
  • Deliverable formats: assumptions & sources register and printable package today; branded lender PDF, editable spreadsheets and data-room export ship before the first cohort is charged
  • Includes: the complete worked sample project, all templates, and six months of rent-benchmark and program updates
  • Sample package preview: a redacted sample package will be published on this page before launch

Beyond the course: graduates with an active property under evaluation can request the scope for Project File Review — a structured review of your submitted file; course tuition may be applied toward an accepted engagement.
