How Much Does It Cost to Build a Sixplex in Toronto? (2026 Numbers)

The short answer: budget $285–300 per square foot on a fixed-price contract with an experienced multiplex builder — about $1.7–1.8 million in construction cost for a typical ~5,900 sq ft six-unit building. The most experienced repeat crews land $265–285; if you've been quoted $350–425/sq ft, you were priced as a custom home. That gap — often half a million dollars on one building — is the difference between a builder doing their first multiplex and a crew doing their ninth.

Why the quotes you're getting are wrong

Call three GTA builders about a six-unit infill and you'll hear numbers between $325 and $425 per square foot. Those numbers aren't dishonest — they're custom-home pricing applied to a building the builder hasn't done before. Custom pricing assumes one-off drawings, unfamiliar assemblies, learning on your dime, and a margin that covers surprises.

A repeatable multiplex template changes the math: same structural system every time, same mechanical layout, trades that have run the sequence before, and materials bought across projects. A capable multiplex builder should sign a fixed-price contract at $285–300 per square foot — and the most experienced repeat teams in the city land $265–285. The fixed price matters more than the number itself: it moves cost-overrun risk (subject to the contract's exclusions and allowances) from your pro forma to the builder's margin.

The full cost stack — construction is only half the project

The build cost is the number everyone asks about, but a sixplex project carries a complete stack. Illustrative figures for a typical project on a standard Toronto lot:

Line Typical range
Land (single-family teardown, sixplex ward) $800k–1.2M+ by pocket
Land transfer tax + closing ~$30–50k
Construction (~5,900 sf @ $285–300/sf fixed) ~$1.7–1.8M
Soft costs (design, permits, energy modelling, application) ~$100k
CMHC MLI Select premium + PST ~$150–190k
Financing costs and interim interest ~$100–125k
Contingency (5% of hard costs) ~$85–90k
All-in, excluding land ~$2.2–2.3M

Scope note: the fixed-price band above typically covers the building itself — structure, envelope, mechanical, finishes. Demolition, shoring, servicing upgrades, appliances, landscaping, permits and HST treatment vary by contract; confirm what's in and out before comparing quotes. Two lines surprise first-timers. The CMHC premium is real cash — the price of admission to the program's financing, and worth every dollar (a topic of its own). And development charges — Toronto has waived DCs for qualifying missing-middle buildings, a five-to-six-figure saving; confirm current treatment for your specific project at permit time.

What actually moves the number

  • Repeatability beats everything. The builder's ninth sixplex costs less than their first — pick teams by their multiplex count, not their renovation portfolio.
  • A fixed-price contract is worth more than a low estimate. An estimate of $260 that becomes $310 is worse than a contract at $285 that stays $285.
  • Design discipline. Full-floor units with minimal corridor space monetize every buildable foot. Complexity — split levels, exotic cladding, one-off assemblies — is where budgets die.
  • The teardown usually helps you. Full demolition means the build itself is new and repeatable — though soils, excavation, servicing, trees and neighbours still carry site risk. Renovating around an existing structure is where the worst horror stories live.
  • Don't max the envelope reflexively. Zoning may allow ~7,000 sq ft; the right building might be 5,900. Every extra foot costs the same ~$285 but rents flatten as units get bigger. Size the building to the debt, not the zoning.

Frequently asked questions

Can you build a sixplex for under $2M all-in (excluding land)? Only at the very efficient end — a repeat crew near $265/sf, tight design, no surprises. For planning, budget the $285–300 band and let a great builder beat it.

Is $285–300/sf realistic in 2026, or a lowball? It's the honest band for an experienced multiplex builder on a repeatable template — the deepest repeat teams report actuals of $265–285. If your builder can't sign fixed-price near this range, they're learning on your project.

What about HST? New purpose-built rentals with 4+ units qualify for the enhanced GST/HST rental rebate — effectively 100% of the HST comes back on a properly structured project. Getting the structure right from day one matters; that's its own article.

How long does it take? Design and permits ~4–8 months (much of it can run during a long closing), construction ~12 months, lease-up ~3–6. Call it two years from offer to stabilized building.
